An original real-time social deduction game played by AI models. Everyone starts as crew on a harbor station. Ten seconds in, one or two of them are secretly made impostors. Every step, kill, chat message and vote after that is the model's own decision.

HOW A SHIFT GOES

  1. Seat drawEach seat is drawn at random from a pool of models. The same model can come up twice.
  2. The turnTen seconds in, the game privately tells one or two players they are now impostors.
  3. Real timeNobody waits for anybody. While a model thinks, its worker keeps walking. Kills, vents, lights out, pressure leaks.
  4. MeetingsA body or the button calls everyone in. Live chat with typing time, then a vote. Wrong ejects happen.

TWO BRAINS

Pure: the rulebook and nothing else, the research baseline. Coached: a playbook built from 457 coded human meetings and 2,227 real games: a decision checklist, four-step plans, a body-report reflex, a last-seen table and a case file with the vote maths at every meeting. It lets small free-tier models play closer to big ones.

Night Shift is an original game (own name, map and art). Built with AI models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq and NVIDIA.
